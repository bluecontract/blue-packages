# How the GoMyOS reseller agreement evolved

The original agreement paid the reseller a percentage of the money the seller actually collected. Edition 19 keeps that rule, including corrections after a refund and separate confirmation of a partner payout. The largest change for the customer is **who authorizes collection, and when**. In the original lesson the customer confirmed satisfaction after delivery. In GoMyOS the two businesses agree on a checkout policy that can collect automatically or wait for the supplier to accept an order or reservation. [Original agreement](https://github.com/bluecontract/myos-core/blob/2a6a72470525e20d6db83e236a6072efa7b7dde6/examples/clause-apps/02-reseller-ledger.blue) · [Edition 19 rules](../examples/sales/declarative/README.md)

**Yes. Go produces this agreement from the ordinary offer configuration.** The seller offers an $80 bicycle rental with a 10% guest discount and a 12.5% reseller commission. ## Changes in behavior

| Decision | October baseline | Current paid agreement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who signs and who sees the terms | The customer, seller, guarantor and partner approve the root agreement. | The supplier and reseller sign a framework agreement. Each customer joins their own order. The operator participates in payment. The effective commission rate for an order stays private to the framework agreement. |
| Who creates an order and sets its price | The customer starts a fixed €100 English lesson from the embedded template. | The partner submits catalogue lines, quantities and the catalogue revision. The agreement calculates the price, freezes the order terms and admits an independently initialized order. It can link the order while supplier approval is pending. |
| What permits collection | The customer's satisfaction confirmation requests capture. The guarantor then confirms the financial outcome. | The customer can authorize the card before supplier approval. Capture requires the active order and its selected payment instruction. A scheduled order also requires the confirmed reservation. An alternative proposed by the supplier requires the customer's acceptance. |
| Which rate applies after a change | The example uses 10% throughout. | Each prepared order retains the agreed rate and price. An amendment affects subsequent orders. A confirmed refund changes accrued commission for that order. A payout that already settled remains a separate historical transaction. |
| What a payout settles | The seller opens one payout for accrued commission, keyed `payout-1`. | The seller chooses an amount from available commission. Each payout has its own key and freezes the gross amount, fee and net transfer. The ledger settles it when the operator's settlement report and the confirmed net transfer match. An unresolved outcome keeps the reservation open. |

The original and current executable examples show the party bindings, collection conditions and accounting rules. [October source](https://github.com/bluecontract/myos-core/blob/2a6a72470525e20d6db83e236a6072efa7b7dde6/examples/clause-apps/02-reseller-ledger.blue) · [Current automatic order](../examples/sales/declarative/automatic-bicycle-rental-agreement.blue) · [Current scheduled order](../examples/sales/declarative/timed-bicycle-rental-agreement.blue)

## Added capabilities

- **Offer configuration.** Go's editor supports up to 250 products, quantities, optional item limits and per-product guest discounts. The agreement uses one of USD, EUR or PLN. Guest discounts and reseller commission have separate rates. The current paid profile can set an agreement-specific minimum commission and can accept a rate below the earlier 5% floor. [Configuration](https://github.com/bluecontract/go-myos/blob/eb7c0ff2c7d018c7ea8b8b79e93816d6fd29486e/server/v2/agreement-drafts/configuration.ts) · [Authoring cases](https://github.com/bluecontract/go-myos/blob/eb7c0ff2c7d018c7ea8b8b79e93816d6fd29486e/tests/stripe-commission-authoring.test.ts)
- **Negotiation and amendments.** The businesses can negotiate the initial rate and exclusivity. Once active, they can jointly approve catalogue and commission amendments separately. A catalogue change has to preserve the supplier and currency and advance the revision by one. Earlier orders retain their own terms. [Amendment declarations](../examples/sales/declarative/README.md#catalog-and-order-inputs)
- **Guest checkout and reservations.** A partner can prepare an order with an account ID or a contact email. The admitted participant mapping establishes the customer's identity. Offers can use automatic confirmation, undated supplier approval or a requested appointment with a supplier-confirmed time. The supplier can propose an alternative for the customer to accept. [Order inputs](../examples/sales/declarative/README.md#catalog-and-order-inputs) · [Reviewed reservation approval](../examples/sales/declarative/README.md#compose-supplier-approval-with-a-reviewed-reservation)
- **Configurable whole-order cancellation.** Offers can omit the policy or set a deadline relative to activation, confirmed service time, or both. When both apply, the earlier cutoff governs, and cancellation at the cutoff is free. The customer can cancel free before required supplier confirmation. The supplier can cancel without a charge. The payment instruction applies the resulting refund or hold release. [Cancellation policy and examples](../examples/sales/declarative/README.md#compose-an-optional-cancellation-policy)
- **Auditable balances.** The agreement exposes confirmed gross receipts, confirmed refunds and net receipts per order and in total. The commission ledger separately tracks accrued, reserved and paid amounts. Both businesses can reconcile an entitlement with the order that earned it and the transfer that settled it. [Receipt and ledger fields](../examples/sales/declarative/README.md#what-an-authorized-reader-can-show)

## Fixes delivered by other engineers

| Failure or edge case | Corrected behavior |
| --- | --- |
| The early catalogue agreement stopped accepting new order preparations after 20 distinct keys. | The agreement-wide ceiling was removed. A new key can start another order and replaying an existing key recovers its preparation. The editor's product-count limit applies to a different quantity. [Preparation limit fix](https://github.com/bluecontract/blue-packages/commit/855b5af9516a33527317dfd0aff676a17fd3c624) |
| A payment guarantor needed a valid identity in an independently started instruction. | Bootstrap supplies the guarantor in the instruction's own participant context and preserves the clause evidence. [Guarantor bootstrap fix](https://github.com/bluecontract/blue-packages/commit/4450d2317b518ace15428e68e7f625991079b4fa) |
| A payment event could arrive before attachment or while agreement access was unavailable. | On attachment, access restoration or acceptance of current terms, the agreement can read the authoritative documents again. Repeated observations converge on the same receipt and commission totals. [Receipt reconciliation](../clause-payment/README.md#reconcile-agreement-receipts-after-access-returns) |
| Payment selection, cancellation and operator responses could arrive in different orders. | The selected instruction must belong to the reviewed order. An unrelated instruction with the same amount is rejected. A late selection can release an existing hold after cancellation. Stable request IDs allow retries, and a refund request accounts for amounts already refunded. [Signed payment scenarios](https://github.com/bluecontract/blue-packages/pull/59) |
| Timing or a late approval could contradict a terminal decision. | The first timed order activation takes precedence over an earlier hold. An automatically activated order without a native timestamp can use the verified time of its original full authorization. Rejection closes approval, and cancellation closes conflicting booking actions. [Clock rules](../examples/sales/declarative/README.md#select-the-start-of-the-elapsed-window) · [Order guards](../blue-orders/README.md) |
| Reopening an older proposal could otherwise substitute newly generated terms. | An unchanged reviewed proposal keeps its exact saved source. New orders use the current catalogue revision, while an identical preparation retry reuses its original terms. [Retained-source tests](https://github.com/bluecontract/go-myos/blob/eb7c0ff2c7d018c7ea8b8b79e93816d6fd29486e/tests/stripe-commission-authoring.test.ts) · [Order preparation](../blue-orders/README.md#prepare-an-independent-order) |

## Architectural assessment

Edition 19 carries forward the strongest design choice in the original example. The order records a commercial decision, the payment instruction records what the operator actually did, and the ledger accounts for what the reseller earned. A request, an authorization, a confirmed capture and a partner payout each answer a different question. The application can show all four states without inventing a financial outcome.

The implementation also moved behavior out of reseller-specific types into shared clauses. `Documents` prepares and links orders, `Commerce` owns catalogue and cancellation rules, `Schedule` owns reservation decisions, `Activation` owns approval, `Payment/Receipts` reads confirmed money movements, and `Ledger` maintains the commission. Go chooses the clauses, participants and conditions for each offer. The main change of direction is commercial. A reusable agreement for two businesses, with individual customer checkouts and configurable collection timing, replaced a single satisfaction-based lesson.
